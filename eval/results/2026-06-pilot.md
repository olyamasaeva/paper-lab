# June 2026 pilot (5 topics, 2 completed)

Superseded by the [goal-aware run](2026-06-goal-aware.md). Kept because its gaps shaped
the skill.

Two of five topics completed (seq2seq + attention, language models; both had HTML
sources). Three failed on a transient API error while a whole PDF was being read into the
context (RecSys w04, NLP w11, NLP w07). Mean score over the two: **7.5 / 10**.

## Gaps that recurred in both topics → changes to the skill

| # | gap | severity | change | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No real training: the student never writes a training loop | high | a late Part "real training": the training step is a stub; a toy or small real corpus, a few hundred CPU steps, assert "loss went down" + generation before/after | applied |
| 2 | No causality or grad-flow tests | high | for autoregressive models: a lookahead test (the future does not affect earlier predictions) and grad flow (param.grad is not None, max ≠ 0) | applied |
| 3 | Too much given, too little to implement | med | the training step and the key architectural module are stubs; only boilerplate (data/IO/plots/metric) is given | applied |
| 4 | Data engineering: padding/masking, BPE, BLEU on real data | med | for sequence tasks: a padding/masking block (masked loss, nothing counted after the first EOS) + an assert on ±1 | applied |
| 5 | A thin bonus | med | an open "Going deeper" block: 2–4 directions + space for an experiment log | applied (without points) |
| 6 | No points per Part | low | skipped: points are deliberately left out | skipped |
| 7 | No debug area, no layered hints | low | a debug cell (forward on dummy input before training) + layered hints instead of full pseudocode in the docstring | applied |

## Where the generator was ahead of the reference

- Numerical asserts (identities, relative error), often missing in the reference.
- Depth of theory and derivations; axes the reference does not touch (Bahdanau vs Luong,
  perplexity bounds, interpolation with λ tuned on dev).
- Inline Questions; "experiments first".
- CPU, seconds per run: an instant edit-check loop.
